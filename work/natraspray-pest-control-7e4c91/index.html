The occupied-house approach

Children. Pets. Plants. Life carries on.

Natraspray's method and natural pest-control products are designed around the concerns people have for their health and environment. The current service message is simple: clients can stay in their homes while the operator works.

The right treatment depends on the pest, property and inspection. A local Natraspray professional can explain the suitable approach.
