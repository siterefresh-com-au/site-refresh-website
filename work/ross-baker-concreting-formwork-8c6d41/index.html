What supports the finish

Work backwards from a clean sign-off.

Every finished surface depends on the work underneath. Ross Baker Concreting manages the sequence from beginning to completion.

  1. PrepareSet-out, levels and the site ready for what follows.
  2. ReinforceSteelwork and supports placed for the planned pour.
  3. FormEdges, falls and dimensions established before concrete arrives.
  4. PourA coordinated crew keeps the placement moving.
  5. FinishThe selected surface is worked cleanly and consistently.

One coordinated pour.

Preparation, steelwork, formwork, pouring and finishing stay connected instead of becoming separate hand-offs.
